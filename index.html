Language Functions • Page 2 & 3

Polite Interruptions & Continuing

Essential workplace etiquette: how to interrupt politely using modal verbs and softeners, and how to resume after an interruption.

✋

Interrupting Politely

6 KEY EXPRESSIONS
"Sorry to interrupt you but …"
Very polite apology softening the interruption.
"Could I interrupt here?"
Polite modal verb 'Could'.
"Do you mind if I say something?"
Indirect polite request for permission.
"Could I just say something?"
Softener 'just' reduces imposition.
"Sorry, I just wanted to say …"
Past form 'wanted' acts as polite distance.
"I have a question."
Direct, neutral; common in webinars.
🔄

Continuing After an Interruption

3 TRANSITION PHRASES
"To get back to what I was saying …"
Clear, professional redirection to the agenda.
"Anyway, as I was saying …"
Friendly resume phrase.
"Anyway …"
Quick informal conversational reset.
Intonation Note: Polite interruptions feature gentle rising pitch (↗) at the end, whereas rude interruptions sound flat or sharply falling (↘).

📐 Grammar Lab: Second Conditional for Unreal Situations & Advice

Slides 5 & 6 • British Council Grammar
THE SECOND CONDITIONAL FORMULA:

If + Past Simple, would + infinitive without to

Time & Reality: Talks about present or future imaginary, hypothetical, or unreal situations (not past facts).
Punctuation Rule: If the if-clause comes first, use a comma ,. If the main clause is first, do NOT use a comma.
Giving Advice: "If I were you, I would (verb)..." uses subjunctive were (instead of was) as the standard formal British advice structure.

🔧 Describing Everyday Objects Formulas (Handout Page 1)

Handout Page 1 & Smartboard Slide 3

Key Patterns for Describing Objects & Tools Without Naming Them

When you don't know the exact English name for a tool or machine, use these British Council descriptive formulas:

RULE 1: VISIBLE MATERIAL

Made of + [material]

Use made of when the original raw material is still visibly intact and recognizable without chemical change.

Examples: "The chair is made of wood." • "The wire cutters are made of steel."
RULE 2: TRANSFORMED MATERIAL

Made from + [material]

Use made from when the original material undergoes transformation, processing, or chemical change into a completely new form.

Examples: "Paper is made from trees." • "Cheese is made from fresh milk."

🗣️ Compound Noun Word Stress Audio Lab

Handout Page 3 & Slide 6
Rule 1: Noun + Noun = 1st Word Stressed
In compounds made of two nouns, the main stress falls heavily on the FIRST word: TEXT message, ANSWERING machine, LAPTOP computer, WHITE house (institution), LIGHT bulb, WIRE cutters, SPIRIT level.
Rule 2: Adjective + Noun = 2nd Word Stressed
In phrases made of an adjective and a noun, the stress falls normally on the SECOND word: mobile PHONE, digital CAMERA, main BRANCH, white HOUSE (any white-painted house).

❓ Indirect Questions Master Lab: The 4 Core Rules

Handout Pages 1 & 2 • Slide 5

The 4 Golden Rules for Forming Polite Indirect Questions

1. Statement Word Order
Word order changes from verb + subject to subject + verb.
"Where is the gate?" → "...where the gate is."
2. Omit Auxiliary 'Do'
Completely drop do, does, or did.
"What time does it leave?" → "...what time it leaves."
3. Insert If / Whether
For Yes/No questions (no question word), add if or whether.
"Is breakfast included?" → "...if breakfast is included."
4. Punctuation Rule
Statements (I wonder..., I'd like to know...) end with a period . not a ?.

💡 Third Conditional Master Lab: Imagined Alternative Past

Handout Page 2 & Notebook Pages 3–4

Third Conditional Formula & Deduction Grammar Architecture

1. If-Clause (Past Condition)
If + Past Perfect (had + V3)
"If I had studied harder..." • "If she hadn't moved abroad..."
2. Result Clause (Hypothetical Past)
would / might / could have + V3
"...I would have passed the examination."
3. Contractions & Weak Forms
In spoken British English, would have becomes would've /wʊdəv/ with weak schwa /ə/.
4. Past Modal of Deduction
must have + V3 (strong certainty based on evidence: "It must have been an easy decision." - ده استنتاج).
